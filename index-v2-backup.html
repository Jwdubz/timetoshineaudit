What your site is costing you — Time to Shine Floor Care —

I'm Jarrett, a Las Vegas web designer. I went through your site the way a homeowner searching for floor cleaning in Las Vegas or Henderson would: find you, trust you, call or book. The foundation is solid: your homepage tested fast in a phone-size load test, it has its own title, description, canonical address and one clear heading, your phone number and a Book Online button are on the first screen, and your sitemap lists 18 pages. The leaks are a header that isn't built for a phone, a homepage with no photo of your work, markup that doesn't say you're a local business, and customer praise that isn't on the page.

Checked October 17, 2026

What I found

01

Your header has no phone version.

The header is one fixed 78-pixel bar that holds your logo and name, four menu links and two buttons (“Call 702-659-8885” and “Book Online”). The page's style sheet has no phone rule for it: the only phone-size rules change the page grid and show the Tap to Call bar at the bottom. In the phone-size capture from my test, the menu words and the two buttons were stacked on top of the page's headline. One capture on one simulated phone, so I can't say how it looks on every phone.

02

The picture on your homepage is a file named logo.png.

The homepage has two images: a 50-pixel logo icon and a file named logo.png that fills the main picture slot, described to screen readers as “Professional floor cleaning service in Las Vegas home”. The same logo.png is the link preview picture (the og:image and twitter:image tags), so a text or post that carries your link shows that file, not a floor. The 50-pixel icon is a 122 KB file, and the test counted about 114 KB of it as wasted.

03

Search tools aren't told you're a local business.

The only markup on the homepage describes a WebSite with a search box. There is no LocalBusiness markup carrying your phone number, service area, hours or reviews. Your phone, your five service areas (Las Vegas, Henderson, Summerlin, Paradise and Green Valley) and your booking link are in the page text, so a person can find them, but a program has to guess.

04

Your customer praise isn't on the page.

The homepage shows three five-star quotes signed “Las Vegas Homeowner”, “Henderson Customer” and “Local Business Owner”, with no names, dates or links. In the searches I ran, the written answers cited a Chamber of Commerce listing at 4.9 from 47 reviews and a Yahoo listing at 5.0 from 76 (figures as the search tool reported them; I didn't open those pages). Your Google profile link is in the footer, but none of those real ratings appear on your own site.

05

Speed is good. A few small things cost you points.

In one phone-size load test the homepage scored 91 for speed, 95 for accessibility, 96 for best practices and 92 for search basics, with the main content showing at 2.9 seconds and 758 KiB transferred. The test flagged images that could be 214 KiB lighter in newer formats, 176 KiB of unused script, nine “Learn More” links (one per service) that don't say what they lead to, and text with too little color contrast. One run on one test setup; field speed is unmeasured.

What's working

Your homepage has its own title, description, canonical address and one clear heading. The phone number and a Book Online button are on the first screen, the services and service areas are spelled out, and your sitemap lists 18 pages: 4 main pages, 9 services and 5 city pages. In my one phone-size load test the speed score was 91 and the main content showed at 2.9 seconds. Your robots.txt allows everything.

Unmeasured

Visitor numbers, rankings, your Google profile and reviews as Google shows them, how any page other than the homepage and sitemap looks or loads (most of my automated requests got a “One moment, please...” waiting page, so I couldn't read them), and whether your Housecall Pro booking works end to end. I didn't book anything or call anyone.

What I checked on your site

  • Found
    Not blocking AI crawlers

    Your robots.txt says “User-agent: *” with an empty Disallow line, which allows every crawler, and it lists your sitemap.

  • Found
    Page text is readable without scripts

    The homepage HTML carries about 540 words of text and one clear heading, so a crawler that doesn't run JavaScript can still read it.

  • Found
    A sitemap covering your pages

    Your sitemap lists 18 pages: the homepage, About, Services and Contact, nine service pages and five city pages.

  • Gap
    No business markup

    The only markup on the homepage is a WebSite entry. There is no LocalBusiness entry with your phone, service area, hours or reviews.

  • Gap
    Link preview shows your logo file

    The og:image and twitter:image tags both point to /images/logo.png, the same file used in the homepage's main picture slot.

  • Note
    Sitemap header is written with https

    The sitemap's opening tag names its standard at https://www.sitemaps.org, and the published standard uses http://. Some readers may be strict about that; I can't say whether Google is. The file also has no dates and was last changed December 2, 2025.

  • Note
    llms.txt shows your homepage

    /llms.txt answers with your homepage's text rather than a separate short guide for AI tools. It is a newer, optional file, so this is a small point.

  • Note
    Footer says 2019–2025

    The footer reads “© 2019–2025 Time to Shine Floor Care LLC”. It is October 2026.

These are plain checks anyone can repeat on October 17, 2026: what the site publishes, not a score.
